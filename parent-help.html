👪 For parents

Help your child use AI as a tutor, not a cheat sheet. Keep them safe.

🛠️Make it: what can I make with AI?Songs, raps, story videos, quizzes, chatbots and more, by age

Do this (3 steps)

  1. Sit with your child the first few times.
  2. Copy a homework prompt below. It tells AI to give hints, not answers.
  3. Ask your child to explain the answer back to you in their own words.

Homework prompts

🧠 Hints, not answers

You are a patient tutor for a [AGE]-year-old. Do NOT give the answer. Ask one question at a time and give small hints until they solve it. The problem is: [PASTE]

📖 Explain it simply

Explain [TOPIC] to a [AGE]-year-old in 5 short sentences with one everyday example. Then ask them one question to check.

🗓️ Practice at home

Make 5 practice questions on [TOPIC] for grade [GRADE], easy to harder. Put the answers at the bottom so I can check.

🛡️ Keep them safe (5 rules)

  1. Check the age: most chatbots are 13+, and Claude is 18+. See each bot's age.
  2. Turn on parental controls if the bot has them (ChatGPT, Gemini via Family Link, Meta AI).
  3. Never share full names, addresses, school names or photos.
  4. AI is a tool, not a friend. No AI "companion" apps for anyone under 18.
  5. If your child is upset or in crisis, talk to them and call or text 988 (US).

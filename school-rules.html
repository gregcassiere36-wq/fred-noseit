📝 Full written steps (text only)
  1. Check the list: Ask IT or your tech coach for the approved-tools list and the district AI policy.
  2. Request a tool: Email IT: tool name, link, what it's for, grade level, whether students log in.
  3. Send the privacy policy: Include the tool's privacy policy and whether it signs a student data privacy agreement.
  4. Wait for approval: Use it with students only after approval. Never use VPNs, proxies or workarounds.
  5. Protect data: No student names, grades or IEP details in AI tools (FERPA). Tools for under-13s need COPPA-compliant consent.

🛡️ Safety tip: Use your own adult account. Never type a child's or student's full name, school, address, grades or photos into an AI tool. Check each tool's age rule before anyone signs up.

Why the rules exist

  • Laws like FERPA and COPPA protect student data.
  • Filters and firewalls are set by your district; we never help bypass them.

2️⃣ How to make it

  1. Read your district's AI policy (ask your principal or tech coach).
  2. If a tool is blocked, don't use VPNs, proxies or personal hotspots to get around it.
  3. Email IT with the template below to request a review.
  4. Meanwhile, use approved tools or prep at home with AI and teach screen-free.

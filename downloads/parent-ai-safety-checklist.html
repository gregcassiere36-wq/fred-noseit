Parent AI Safety Checklist

A one-page check before and while your child uses AI · Fred Noseit (aiedume.ai) · Free

Before they start

  • I checked the tool's age rule (many say 13+, some need a parent's OK).
  • I set up the account myself, or with my child.
  • I turned on safety or family settings where the app has them.
  • I know what the school allows for homework.
  • We agreed when and where AI is used (for example, in the kitchen, not the bedroom).

Never share (teach this out loud)

  • Full name, address, school name or phone number.
  • Photos of themselves, family or friends.
  • Passwords or codes.
  • Anything they wouldn't want a stranger to read.

While they use it

  • AI is a helper that can be wrong. We check one fact each time.
  • We ask for hints, not answers, for homework.
  • If something feels weird, scary or rude, close it and tell an adult.
  • We take breaks and keep screen time balanced.

Talk about it every week

  • “What did you ask AI this week?”
  • “Did it get anything wrong?”
  • “When is it OK to use AI for school, and when is it cheating?”

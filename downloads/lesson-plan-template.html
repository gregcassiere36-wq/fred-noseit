Lesson steps

PartMinutesWhat happens
Hook5Play the song once. Students listen for the 3 key ideas.
Teach10Pause after each verse. Explain the idea; students write it down.
Practise15Groups write a new verse or act out the chorus.
Check5Exit ticket: 3 questions from the song.

Before class checklist

  • I checked every fact in the lyrics.
  • Lyrics are original (not copying a real song).
  • Words fit the grade's reading level.
  • No student names or photos used in AI tools.
  • I have captions or printed lyrics for students who need them.

Prompt to copy

Write original song lyrics that teach [3 key ideas] to grade [x] in a [pop/rap/folk] style. Verse, chorus, verse, chorus. Then list every fact so I can check it.

Reflection

What worked? What will I change?

AI can be wrong. Check facts before you use them with students or children. Never type a child's full name, address, school, photo or grades into an AI tool.

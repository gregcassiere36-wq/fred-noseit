1. Why we use AI

We use AI as a helper for learning: to get ideas, practise and check our thinking. It does not replace our own work.

3. If you use AI, say so

Add one line to your work: “I used [tool] to [what you used it for].”

4. Privacy

  • Never type your full name, address, photos or passwords.
  • Use school-approved tools and accounts only.

5. AI can be wrong

Check facts with a book, a trusted website or the teacher. AI detectors are not reliable, so we talk it through instead of guessing.

6. What happens if a rule is broken

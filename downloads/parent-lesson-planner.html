Ideas to fill the week

  • Read together for 15 minutes, then ask 3 questions (AI can suggest the questions).
  • Kitchen maths: measure a recipe, double it.
  • Ask AI for a riddle on today's topic and solve it together.
  • Draw what you learned, then explain it to a family member.
  • Make a 5-question quiz for a parent to answer. Your child is the teacher!

🏪 The Plugin Store

Real teachers and parents sell plugins made from their own know-how. Search by topic, by grade (PreK to 12th), or by ZIP code to find sellers near you.

Every plugin page shows a short intro and an example before you buy. Some plugins give free sample chats first — the seller picks how many, up to 20. Prices can be per month, per year, or forever.

💰 Sell what you know

Build a plugin with the Plugin Helper, step by step. You keep 80% of every sale. There is a $1 one-time posting fee for each plugin.

You can give free samples (up to 20 chats), or make your plugin free.

🔗 Use your plugin in other AIs

Your plugin can work in ChatGPT, Muse, Grok Bot, or any AI chat. Go to My Plugins and tap Connect on your plugin. Copy your key and the starter text. Paste them into the other AI chat — your plugin is working there.

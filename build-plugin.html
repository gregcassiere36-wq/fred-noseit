What a “plugin” (Gem) can do
1️⃣ What it can do
  • A Gem is a saved AI helper with your instructions built in, so you don't retype them.
  • Examples: Rubric Builder, Parent Email Writer, Exit Ticket Maker, Lyric Coach.
  • You can share it with a link (people need a Google account).
2️⃣ How to make it
  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari or Edge). Click the address bar at the top, type gemini.google.com and press Enter.
  2. Click Sign in (top right) and use your Google account. A school account works if your district allows Gemini.
  3. On the left side, click Explore Gems (or Gems), then New Gem.
  4. Type a name, e.g. Exit Ticket Maker.
  5. In Instructions, paste the template below and fill in the [brackets].
  6. Optional: click + / add files to upload a rubric or template. Nothing private.
  7. Use the preview on the right: test it with 5 real requests.
  8. Fix the instructions until results are good, then click Save.
  9. To share: open the Gem list, click ⋯ next to your Gem → Share.
Gem instructions templateYou are [name], a helper for [grade/subject] teachers. When the teacher gives you [input], you return [output] in this format: [format]. Always: use plain language, match grade level, add an answer key. Never: invent facts, ask for student names or private data. If unsure, say so.

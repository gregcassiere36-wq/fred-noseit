🤖 Chatbots: pick one

Not sure? Teachers: start with MagicSchool or Khanmigo. Parents: start with Gemini or ChatGPT. Songs: Suno.

💬 Everyday chatbots🏫 School & kid tools🎵 Songs & videos🤖 Make your own

💬 Everyday chatbots

🤖ChatGPTWriting, explaining, lesson ideas, a good first pick. · 13+🤖Gemini (Google)Anyone who already uses Gmail, Google Docs or Google Classroom. · 13+ on their own🤖ClaudeCareful writing, long documents and planning for adults. · Adults only: 18+🤖Microsoft CopilotSchools that use Microsoft, Windows and Edge users. · 13+ (18+ in Australia)🤖GrokQuick answers and current news, inside X or on its own. · 13+🤖Meta AIFamilies already on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook. · Teen accounts are 13–17 (14–17 in some places)🤖PerplexityResearch with sources, a good fit for checking facts. · 13+

🏫 School & kid tools

🤖Khanmigo (Khan Academy)Lesson plans, rubrics and exit tickets for teachers, plus a hints-first tutor. · Free for teachers🤖MagicSchool80+ teacher tools: lesson plans, quizzes, rubrics, rewrites, emails. · Free teacher plan🤖SchoolAITeacher chat (called Dot) and student “Spaces”, which are safe class chatbots you set up. · Free for teachers with no end date🤖Brisk TeachingAI inside Google Docs, Slides and YouTube: rewrite levels, quizzes, feedback. · Free Chrome and Edge extension for teachers (20+ tools, with a usage limit)🤖Eduaide120+ kinds of teaching resources: worksheets, games, lesson plans. · Free: 24 credits a month (about 8 lesson plans)🤖Google Lens (replaces Socratic)Snap a photo of a homework problem to get explanations and step-by-step help. · Free in the Google app

🎵 Songs & videos

🤖Canva (Magic Studio)Slides, posters, worksheets and simple videos, with AI picture and writing helpers. · Canva's AI is 13+, unless used inside Canva Education under a teacher🤖Suno (AI songs)Turning lyrics into a full song in any style, for lesson songs and raps. · 13+🤖Udio (AI songs)Trying out song ideas. Listening and sharing a link only. · 16+🤖HeyGen (talking avatars)A talking-avatar welcome or explainer video. · Adults only: 18+🤖CapCut (video editing)Putting a song and pictures together into a video. · 13+ (16+ in some places)

🤖 Make your own class chatbot

  1. Free: make a Gemini Gem at gemini.google.com → Gems → New Gem.
  2. Schools: use a SchoolAI Space so you can see what students ask.
  3. Or a ChatGPT GPT (creating one needs a paid plan) or Grok with custom instructions.

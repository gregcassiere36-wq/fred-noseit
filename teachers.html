Top 5 teacher tasks

These are the things teachers use AI for most (Gallup, 2025).

📝 Plan a lesson

Act as an experienced [GRADE] teacher. Write a 45-minute lesson plan on [TOPIC] with a warm-up, a hands-on activity, a check for understanding and an exit ticket. Use simple language.

❓ Make a quiz

Write a 10-question quiz on [TOPIC] for [GRADE]. Mix multiple choice and short answer. Put the answer key at the end.

🧩 Adapt for every student

Rewrite this reading passage at three levels: below grade, on grade and above grade for [GRADE]. Keep the key facts the same. Passage: [PASTE]

✅ Build a rubric

Make a simple 4-level rubric for [ASSIGNMENT] with 4 criteria. Use kid-friendly words a [GRADE] student can understand.

✉️ Write a parent email

Write a short, warm email to parents about [TOPIC/EVENT]. Under 120 words. Friendly, clear, no jargon. Include one thing parents can do at home.

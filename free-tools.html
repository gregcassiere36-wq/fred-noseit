Quick guide

1️⃣ What it can do

  • Gemini (gemini.google.com): general helper, free with a Google account, 13+.
  • MagicSchool: 80+ ready-made teacher tools, free plan.
  • Brisk Teaching: Chrome extension that works inside Google Docs, free for educators.
  • Khanmigo for teachers: free teacher tools from Khan Academy.

2️⃣ How to make it

  1. Pick one tool. Go to its website.
  2. Click Sign up and use your school email if your district allows it.
  3. Try one task you do every week.
  4. Write down what saved time.
  5. Check if it's on your district's approved list before students use it.

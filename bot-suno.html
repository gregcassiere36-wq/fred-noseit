🧰 Suno (AI songs)

Turning lyrics into a full song in any style, for lesson songs and raps.

💵 Cost: Free: 50 credits a day (about 10 songs). Free songs are for personal, non-commercial use.

🎂 Age: 13+. Ages 13–17 need a parent's consent.

Get started (3 steps)

  1. Go to suno.com.
  2. Sign up, click Create, paste your lyrics.
  3. Try one of the prompts below, then check the result.

3 prompts to try

Write song lyrics for 2nd graders about the water cycle. 2 verses and a chorus.

Make it upbeat pop, easy to sing.

Make a slower, calm version for the end of the day.

🛡️ Safety

Teachers: use your own adult account and play songs in class. Never type private info about students or kids.

Renting vs. buying

1️⃣ What it can do

  • Rent: a teacher pays a small weekly or monthly price to use your helper.
  • Buy: a one-time price for a copy of your instructions/template.
  • You set your own price. You can offer one or both.

2️⃣ How to make it

  1. Build your helper (see Build a Plugin).
  2. Click ➕ Post your plugin.
  3. Fill in name, subject, grade, what it does.
  4. Type a Rent price, a Buy price, or both.
  5. Paste your YouTube demo link.
  6. Preview your card. Today it saves only on your own device (demo).

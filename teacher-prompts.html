2️⃣ How to make it

👉 Show me the steps
1
Pick a prompt

Look at the prompts below.

2
Click Copy this

Click the 📋 Copy this button next to it.

3
Open the AI website

Go to gemini.google.com.

4
Paste it

Click the box. Press Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V).

5
Fill in the [brackets]

Change [topic] and [x] to your topic and grade. Press Enter.

🎉 You did it! Great job.

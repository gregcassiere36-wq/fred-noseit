A free one-page guide for parents. What it is: seven simple habits for using AI at home. Why it helps: your child gets a patient helper that asks questions, while you stay in charge of safety.

  1. Tell the AI to act as a tutor that asks questions, not one that gives answers.
  2. Sit with your child, especially if they are under 13. Check each tool's age rule.
  3. Never share names, schools, addresses or photos.
  4. Check one fact from every AI answer in a book or trusted site.
  5. Keep sessions short; mix in offline practice.
  6. Make a learning song together with a free tool.
  7. Ask the teacher about the school's AI rules.

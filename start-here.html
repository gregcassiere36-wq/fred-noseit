Day 1–2: Get comfortable

1️⃣ What it can do

  • Ask questions in plain English, like texting a helpful colleague.
  • Ask it to make something shorter, simpler or friendlier.
  • Keep asking follow-ups: “Make it shorter.” “Add an example.”

2️⃣ How to make it

  1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Safari or Edge). Click the address bar at the top, type gemini.google.com and press Enter.
  2. Click Sign in (top right) and use your Google account. A school account works if your district allows Gemini.
  3. Click the box that says Ask Gemini. Click 📋 Copy this below, then click the box and press Ctrl+V (Mac: Cmd+V). Press Enter.
  4. After the answer appears, type a follow-up such as Make it shorter and press Enter.
  5. Read the answer. AI can be wrong, so check at least one fact in a book or trusted website before you use it.
Try a follow-upRewrite that in simpler words, under 80 words, for a busy parent.

Day 3–5: Save real time

1️⃣ What it can do

  • Draft a parent email, a quiz or a lesson outline in about a minute.
  • Turn your notes into a printable worksheet.
  • Get three ideas when you feel stuck.

2️⃣ How to make it

  1. Pick one task you already do every week.
  2. Open gemini.google.com and sign in.
  3. Copy one of the prompts below, paste it, and fill in the [brackets].
  4. Edit the result so it sounds like you.
  5. Save it: click the copy icon under the answer and paste it into Google Docs or Word.
Weekly taskI spend time every week on [task]. Give me a step-by-step way to do it faster, and a template I can reuse.

Golden safety rules

1️⃣ What it can do

  • Protects student privacy.
  • Keeps you inside school rules.
  • Keeps you in charge of every result.

2️⃣ How to make it

  1. Never type student names, grades, health or family details.
  2. Use tools your school approves for anything students touch.
  3. Check facts before you share.
  4. You make the final decision. AI only drafts.

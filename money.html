What you can sell (and what you can't)

1️⃣ What it can do

  • Your own original lessons, routines, printables, rubrics and helpers.
  • Workshops and tutoring based on your experience.

2️⃣ How to make it

  1. Only sell what you created. Don't resell copyrighted textbooks or another teacher's work.
  2. Check your teaching contract or district policy: some schools own work made on school time.
  3. Check each tool's rules (for example, Suno's free plan is non-commercial).
  4. Never include student work, names or photos without written permission.
  5. Price fairly and write a clear refund rule.

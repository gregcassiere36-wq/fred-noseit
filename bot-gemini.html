🤖 Gemini (Google)

Anyone who already uses Gmail, Google Docs or Google Classroom.

💵 Cost: Free. Paid Google AI plans exist.

🎂 Age: 13+ on their own. Under 13 only with a parent-managed Family Link account (US; not in the UK or EU).

Sign up (3 steps)

  1. Go to gemini.google.com or get the app.
  2. Sign in with your Google account (kids under 13: a parent sets it up in Family Link).
  3. Type a question and press Enter. That's it.

3 prompts to try

Explain what Gemini (Google) can and can't do, in 5 simple bullet points for a beginner.

I'm a [teacher/parent] of a [AGE]-year-old. Give me 3 ways to use you this week to save time. Keep it short.

Check this for mistakes and tell me what to double-check: [PASTE]

🛡️ Safety

In Family Link: pick your child → Controls → Gemini Apps → on or off. Never type private info about students or kids.

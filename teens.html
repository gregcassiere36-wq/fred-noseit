Set up a study coach

Open gemini.google.com with your teen. Paste this.

Copy thisYou are a study coach for my teen in grade [x]. Quiz them on [topic], one question at a time. Don't give the answer until they try.
3
Let them try

4
Talk about it

Ask what they learned. Remind them AI can be wrong.
